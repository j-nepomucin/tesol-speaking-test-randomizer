# B1 Speaking Topic Randomizer

A topic randomizer for **Part 4 Speaking** of my B1 EFL Midterm Examination.

**Live page:** https://j-nepomucin.github.io/tesol-speaking-test-randomizer/

## What it does
- **Test 1 · Q&A Dialogue:** shows 3 random topic cards for one student. The teacher clicks to show the next 3 cards for the partner.
- **Test 2 · Short Talk:** shows 1 random topic card (a mind-map with 4 points) for one student at a time.
- Everything is random: who starts, the cards, the order of the talks, and the topics. No card repeats within a group.
- Works for pairs and groups of 3. **Reset group** makes a new random draw and sets the group count back to 0.

## How to use
Open the live page on the classroom screen. Choose Pair or Group of 3, then follow the buttons.
